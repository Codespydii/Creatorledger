Most creators get blindsided by taxes for one reason: the money hits your account in full, with nothing withheld. Unlike a salaried job, no one is quietly setting aside a chunk for the tax authority. That part is on you.

Here is a system that works no matter how lumpy your income is.

## Pick a percentage and move it on payday

The simplest rule that actually works: every time you get paid, immediately move a fixed percentage into a separate savings account. For many creators, **25–30%** is a safe starting point, but the right number depends on where you live and how much you earn.

The key is *automation by habit*: the transfer happens the moment money lands, before you mentally count it as spendable.

## Track every deductible expense

The flip side of taxes is deductions. Every legitimate business expense lowers the income you owe tax on — cameras, software, your home-office share, travel to shoots, and more.

The problem is that creators lose receipts. The fix is to capture them in the moment. Caelo's [AI receipt scanner](/features/ai-receipt-scanner) reads the vendor, amount, date and category from a photo, so your write-offs are logged the second you spend.

## Know your real number, not your gross

Gross revenue is a vanity metric at tax time. What matters is profit — revenue minus expenses — across every income stream.

If you earn from AdSense, sponsorships, affiliate links and merch, a spreadsheet falls apart fast. [Income tracking](/features/income-tracking) built for creators keeps all of it in one ledger so your taxable profit is always accurate.

## Forecast the lumps

Creator income is seasonal and often paid late — AdSense, for example, pays roughly two months in arrears. If you only look at your current balance, you will misjudge what you can set aside.

A [90-day cash forecast](/features/cash-forecast) shows what is actually landing and when, so you can size your tax transfers against real future income instead of guessing.

## The five-minute monthly routine

1. Reconcile the month's income and expenses.
2. Confirm your tax-savings transfers happened.
3. Scan any stray receipts you missed.
4. Glance at the next 90 days.
5. Close the laptop.

Do this every month and tax season stops being a crisis. It becomes a non-event.
